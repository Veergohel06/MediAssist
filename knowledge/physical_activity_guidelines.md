# Global Recommendations on Physical Activity for Health

## Definition and Scope
Physical activity refers to all bodily movement produced by skeletal muscles that requires energy expenditure. This includes exercise as well as physical activities undertaken while working, active transportation (walking, cycling), doing household chores, and participating in recreational sports.

## Recommended Levels for Adults (Aged 18–64 Years)
To achieve substantial health benefits and reduce the risks of all-cause mortality, cardiovascular diseases, hypertension, type 2 diabetes, depression, anxiety, and cognitive decline, the WHO recommends:
- **Aerobic Activity:**
  - At least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity per week (e.g., brisk walking, easy cycling, dancing, active gardening); OR
  - At least 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity per week (e.g., running, fast cycling, lap swimming, high-intensity interval training); OR
  - An equivalent combination of moderate- and vigorous-intensity activity throughout the week.
- **Muscle-Strengthening Activities:**
  - Muscle-strengthening activities involving all major muscle groups (legs, hips, back, abdomen, chest, shoulders, and arms) on 2 or more days per week.
- **Limiting Sedentary Behavior:**
  - Replace sedentary time (prolonged sitting, screen time) with physical activity of any intensity, including light intensity. Any physical activity is significantly better than none.

## Older Adults (Aged 65 Years and Above)
- Older adults should follow the same adult aerobic and muscle-strengthening guidelines, with an added emphasis on **multicomponent functional balance and strength training** at moderate or greater intensity on 3 or more days per week.
- Balance and coordination exercises help prevent falls and reduce fall-related fractures and injuries.

## Health Benefits of Regular Movement
- Enhances muscular fitness, joint mobility, and bone density.
- Lowers risk of stroke, coronary heart disease, and hypertension.
- Improves insulin sensitivity and glycemic control.
- Enhances mental wellbeing, alleviates stress, and promotes deeper nocturnal sleep quality.
